# 🎮 Buzz Streaks & Gamification - Quick Start Guide

## 🔥 What Are Streaks?

**Streaks** track how many consecutive days you've buzzed with each friend. The longer your streak, the higher your rank!

### How Streaks Work:
- 📅 **Buzz daily** with a friend to maintain your streak
- 🔥 **Streak counter** appears on their bee avatar
- ❄️ **Streak Freeze**: Miss one day? Your streak is saved automatically (one-time use)
- ⚠️ **Streak Resets**: Miss 2+ days without a freeze → streak resets to 0

### Streak Rules:
1. Buzz a friend today → Streak starts at 1
2. Buzz them again tomorrow → Streak increases to 2
3. Keep buzzing daily → Streak keeps growing
4. Miss a day → Freeze activates (if available)
5. Miss 2 days → Streak resets

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: How do I start a streak?**
A: Just buzz a friend! Your streak starts at 1 and grows each consecutive day.

**Q: What happens if I forget to buzz for a day?**
A: Your streak freeze activates automatically (one-time use). Buzz again the next day to continue!

**Q: Can I have multiple streaks?**
A: Yes! You can have a separate streak with each friend.

**Q: How do I know my streak is about to break?**
A: Check the last buzz date in your chat history. If it's been 2+ days, your streak will reset.

**Q: Do achievements give rewards?**
A: Achievements are badges of honor! They show your dedication and appear on the leaderboard.

**Q: How often does the leaderboard update?**
A: The leaderboard updates in real-time as users send buzzes and unlock achievements.

**Q: Can I lose achievements?**
A: No! Once unlocked, achievements are permanent.

**Q: What time does the streak day reset?**
A: Streaks are based on calendar days (UTC timezone). Buzz anytime within a 24-hour period.
